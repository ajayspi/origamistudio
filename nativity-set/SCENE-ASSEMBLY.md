# Scene Assembly: Creating the Full 3D Nativity

This guide focuses on composition - making it a unified scene, not just a collection of figures.

## Pre-Assembly Planning

Before folding anything, plan your composition. The key to a dramatic nativity is creating depth and storytelling through placement.

### Composition Ideas
- **Classic but dynamic**: Holy Family in recessed alcove, animals around, shepherd nearby, Wise Men approaching from outside
- **Architectural focus**: Emphasize the stable structure with figures at different depths
- **Layered**: Put some figures inside the stable depth, others at the entryway

## Step 1: Build the Architectural Stable

1. **Base** - Create a 8-10" display base. Cover with light tan paper for ground.
2. **Back wall** - Angle slightly, create recess for manger
3. **Side walls** - Angle inward (not 90°) creating cozy alcove
4. **Manger** - Build recessed, layered structure separate from walls
5. **Ground** - Add texture, create slight slope or different levels
6. **Roof** - Layered thatch, overhanging to create shadows
7. **Details** - Add small architectural elements, keep it open-front

## Step 2: Create the Holy Family (Heart of Scene)

Position in the rear alcove, deepest part of the stable:

1. **Manger** - Place in center-back recess
2. **Baby Jesus** - Nest in manger
3. **Mary** - Kneel adjacent to manger, angled toward it (creates intimacy)
4. **Joseph** - Stand behind/slightly to the side, protective stance angled toward family
5. **Lighting** - Position so they catch "focus" in the deepest part

## Step 3: Add Animals

Place naturally, not symmetrically:

1. **Donkey/Ox** - Near entrance or outside, suggesting travel
2. **Sheep** - Scattered, some near shepherd, some resting
3. **Positioning** - Don't line them up, cluster naturally

## Step 4: Shepherd and Flock

1. **Shepherd** - Position outside near entrance or just inside, looking toward Holy Family
2. **Sheep** - Around him, integrated naturally
3. **Pose** - Crouched/kneeling brings him to a lower level, creates depth variation

## Step 5: Wise Men Approach

Create a sense of journey:

1. **Wise Man 1** - Farthest back or approaching, tallest robes
2. **Wise Man 2** - Mid-distance, different pose
3. **Wise Man 3** - Closest to stable, bowing
4. **Path** - Create a subtle paper path leading to stable
5. **Gifts** - Position their offerings toward the manger

## Step 6: Angel and Star

Create vertical drama:

1. **Angel** - Above and behind the stable, soaring pose pointing toward/starward
2. **Star** - Above, angled to draw the eye
3. **Height** - Use risers or position to create vertical composition

## Composition Tips

- **Depth is key**: Place some figures deep in alcove, some at front edge
- **Create triangles**: Group elements in triangular compositions for stability
- **Vary levels**: Kneeling, crouching, standing, soaring creates interest
- **Lead the eye**: Star draws up, then down to angel, to stable, to Holy Family
- **Negative space**: Don't fill every inch - empty space creates breathing room
- **Lighting**: The 3D forms cast shadows; position for dramatic effect
- **Storytelling**: Show the journey (Wise Men approaching) not just arrival

## Final Touches

- **Subtle shaping**: After assembly, gently curve robes and wings with fingers
- **Adjust poses**: Tiny adjustments make big difference
- **Check stability**: Ensure all pieces stand
- **View from different angles**: Rotate and adjust until it looks good from front and sides
- **Less perfect**: Organic shapes look more natural than perfectly symmetrical

## Display Suggestions

- Use a shallow tray with raised edges
- Add subtle ground texture
- Consider lighting from the side to enhance shadows
- The scene should tell the story of the Nativity
