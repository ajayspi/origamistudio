# Additional Origami Nativity Resources

## Online Tutorials Found
- eHow: "Origami Nativity Scene Instructions" - has steps for Mary, Jesus/manger, sheep (diagrams referenced but text only)
- Origami Spirit (Leyla Torres): Multi-part series - Joseph, Mary, Child/crib (video-based, Spanish/English)
- Shoo Rayner: Draw-and-make nativity set with printable templates/videos (12 days series)
- YouTube: "Easy Origami Christmas Nativity Mother Mary Tutorial!" and others

## Recommended Books
- "A complete Origami Nativity" by Peter Buchan-Symons - covers 30+ original models, advanced techniques (Origami-shop.com)

## Free/Accessible Options
- Origami.me nativity-like models: Angel, Box (manger/stable), 5-pointed star, animals
- Combine individual models into set (easier than finding full pre-made nativity)

## Practical Approach
For a complete set, combine:
1. Stable/Manger: Masu box (origami.me/box)
2. Holy Family: Custom robe folds or adapt figures
3. Angel/Star: origami.me/angel, origami.me/5-pointed-star
4. Animals/Shepherds: origami.me/origami-animals + robe figures
5. Wise Men: Robe figures in rich colors

## Notes
Many full nativity sets are sold as books/PDFs. The free tutorials are often split across multiple pages/videos. Our combined guide in nativity-set/ is practical for folding from home.
