Goal

A freestanding, angled stable with a recessed manger alcove to house the Holy Family. Focus on depth and architecture.

Materials Needed

Brown Kraft / Textured Brown 6" x 6" × 2
Darker Brown 6" x 6" × 1
Light Tan 6" x 6" × 1
Bone Folder (Recommended) Tool

Step By Step Instructions

1

Create The Base Platform

⏱️ 3-5 minutes

Take your light tan 6" square. Fold a small tab along the bottom edge (~1"). This tab will lock your walls later and keep the whole stable stable on your display tray.

💡 Pro Tip: Don't make this tab too big. A thin, crisp tab is stronger than a wide floppy one.
Done
Checked Fit
2

Fold The Back Wall

⏱️ 4-6 minutes

Take your first brown 6" square. Crease lengthwise. Instead of cutting a perfect triangle, angle the top corners inwards at roughly 25-30 degrees to create a sloped stable roofline. Leave room for the alcove to recess behind it.

💡 Pro Tip: Keep it slightly taller in the middle. Subtle asymmetry looks far more architectural than perfectly symmetrical.
Done
Angles Match
3

Shape The Angled Side Walls

⏱️ 6-8 minutes

Take your second brown 6" square and fold down to ~5.5". Create a base tab (~0.75"). Angle the top edge to match your back wall slope. Do NOT fold these at 90 degrees. Angle them in towards each other to form a cozy, recessed alcove.

💡 Pro Tip: Test fit as you go. If you can see straight through the middle with no recess, angle them in more.
Done
Angled Inward
Test Fitted
